# BookSwipe — Screenshot Guide

> Guidelines for creating App Store and Google Play screenshots.

---

## Required Sizes

### Apple App Store

| Device | Size (px) | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| iPhone 6.9" (16 Pro Max) | 1320 × 2868 | ✅ Yes |
| iPhone 6.7" (15 Plus / 14 Pro Max) | 1290 × 2796 | ✅ Yes |
| iPhone 6.5" (11 Pro Max) | 1242 × 2688 | ✅ Yes |
| iPhone 5.5" (8 Plus) | 1242 × 2208 | ✅ Yes |
| iPad Pro 12.9" (6th gen) | 2048 × 2732 | Optional |
| iPad Pro 12.9" (2nd gen) | 2048 × 2732 | Optional |

- **Format:** PNG or JPEG, no alpha
- **Count:** Up to 10 per device size; minimum 3 recommended
- **Orientation:** Portrait recommended

### Google Play Store

| Type | Size (px) | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| Phone | Min 320px, max 3840px (16:9 or 9:16) | ✅ Yes (2-8 screenshots) |
| 7" Tablet | 1024 × 600 minimum | Optional |
| 10" Tablet | 1920 × 1200 minimum | Optional |

- **Format:** PNG or JPEG, 24-bit, no alpha
- **Recommended phone size:** 1080 × 1920 or 1440 × 2560

## Screenshot Sequence (8 Screenshots)

### 1. Swipe Discovery (Hero Shot)

- **Screen:** Main swipe screen with a book card visible
- **State:** A book card mid-swipe with a slight right tilt, showing the "like" indicator
- **Caption:** **"Swipe Right on Your Next Favorite Book"**
- **Notes:** This is the hero screenshot — make it pop. Show a recognizable, visually appealing book cover.

### 2. Book Detail

- **Screen:** Book detail page after tapping a card
- **State:** Full book info visible — cover, title, author, rating, description, reviews summary
- **Caption:** **"Get the Full Story Before You Commit"**
- **Notes:** Show a book with a 4.5-star rating and a couple of review snippets visible.

### 3. Category Browser

- **Screen:** Category/genre filter screen
- **State:** Grid of category chips (Fiction, Sci-Fi, Romance, Mystery, etc.) with 2-3 selected
- **Caption:** **"Explore Every Genre You Love"**
- **Notes:** Show a colorful grid with varied category names.

### 4. Liked Books

- **Screen:** Liked books list/grid
- **State:** Grid view with 8-12 book covers showing variety
- **Caption:** **"Your Liked Books, All in One Place"**
- **Notes:** Show a healthy collection to suggest the app is well-used.

### 5. Reading Lists

- **Screen:** Reading lists overview
- **State:** 3-4 custom lists visible ("Beach Reads," "Sci-Fi Marathon," "2026 Goals") with cover thumbnails
- **Caption:** **"Organize Your Reading Your Way"**
- **Notes:** Show named lists with book count badges.

### 6. Reviews

- **Screen:** Book reviews screen (on a book detail page, reviews section)
- **State:** 2-3 user reviews visible with star ratings and review text
- **Caption:** **"Read Reviews. Share Your Thoughts."**
- **Notes:** Show a mix of 4-5 star reviews with realistic snippet text.

### 7. Search & Discover

- **Screen:** Search screen with results
- **State:** Search bar with a query typed, results grid below
- **Caption:** **"Find Any Book in Seconds"**
- **Notes:** Show a common search term like "fantasy" with relevant results.

### 8. Premium / Subscription

- **Screen:** Premium upgrade screen or paywall
- **State:** Feature comparison (free vs premium) with the Premium benefits highlighted
- **Caption:** **"Go Premium. Unlimited Discovery."**
- **Notes:** Show the value proposition clearly — unlimited swipes, advanced filters, no ads.

## Design Guidelines

### Device Frames
- Use Apple and Google's official device frame assets
- iPhone 15 Pro frame (titanium) for iOS screenshots
- Pixel 8 frame for Android screenshots
- Consistent frame color across all screenshots

### Marketing Text Overlay
- Place caption text **above** the device frame (top 20% of the image)
- Font: Bold sans-serif (SF Pro Display or Inter), white text
- Optional subtle drop shadow for readability
- Background: Extend the app's gradient (indigo→purple) behind the device frame
- Keep text large enough to read in the App Store thumbnail grid

### Composition
- Device frame centered, occupying ~70% of the image height
- Consistent padding and alignment across all screenshots
- Optional: subtle confetti, sparkle, or book illustrations as background accents (keep minimal)

### Color Palette
- Background gradient: #4A00E0 → #8E2DE2 (match app icon)
- Text: White (#FFFFFF)
- Accent: #FFD700 (gold) for highlights or stars
